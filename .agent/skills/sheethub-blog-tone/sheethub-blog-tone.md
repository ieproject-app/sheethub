# Skill: sheethub-blog-tone

> Voice, tone, and narrative standards for all SheetHub blog posts. Must be read alongside `content-generator` before writing any article.

## 1. Tone Checklist
- Clear, practical, step-by-step
- Professional yet approachable
- English language only
- Includes practical formula examples
- Ends with a warm, helpful summary ("SheetHub's Take")
