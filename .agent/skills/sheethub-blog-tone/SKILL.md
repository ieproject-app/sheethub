# SheetHub Blog Writing Tone & Style Guide

This skill defines the writing standard for all SheetHub blog articles — ensuring every post is clear, practical, expert yet approachable, and focused on spreadsheet mastery (Excel & Google Sheets).

---

## Core Writing Principles

1. **Direct Problem Statement**: Start the introduction with a real spreadsheet frustration or scenario before explaining the solution.
2. **Authoritative Yet Approachable Voice**: Speak like an experienced spreadsheet practitioner. Avoid artificial hype, robotic transitions (*"Furthermore", "In conclusion"*), and fluffy preamble.
3. **Pacing & Scannability**: Use clean `##` and `###` heading hierarchy, practical data tables, bullet points for lists, and syntax blocks.
4. **Concrete Examples**: Always provide realistic sample data or scenarios rather than generic `foo/bar` placeholders.
5. **Clean Prose**: Avoid excessive em-dashes and AI clichés. Ensure natural readability.
