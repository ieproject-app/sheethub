Excel shortcuts are the fastest way to boost your productivity. One key combination can save you 5 seconds. Fifty shortcuts save you minutes every hour. Over a year, that is hours saved.

Here is every shortcut you actually need, organized by what you are trying to do.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Windows vs Mac">
  Windows shortcuts use <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd>. Mac shortcuts use <Kbd>Cmd</Kbd>. Where they differ, both are listed.
</Callout>

## Navigation

| Action | Windows | Mac |
|--------|---------|-----|
| Move to edge of data region | <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + Arrow | <Kbd>Cmd</Kbd> + Arrow |
| Go to beginning of sheet | <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>Home</Kbd> | <Kbd>Cmd</Kbd> + <Kbd>Fn</Kbd> + <Kbd>Left</Kbd> |
| Move between sheets | <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>PgUp/PgDn</Kbd> | <Kbd>Cmd</Kbd> + <Kbd>PgUp/PgDn</Kbd> |

| Move to next worksheet | <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>Tab</Kbd> | <Kbd>Cmd</Kbd> + <Kbd>Tab</Kbd> |

## Pro Tips

**Learn two shortcuts at a time.** Trying to memorize 50 at once is overwhelming. Pick two that solve your current pain point, use them until they are automatic, then add two more.

**Customize the Quick Access Toolbar.** Right-click any button → Add to Quick Access Toolbar. Assign shortcuts with <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + number.

**Use the search bar.** In modern Excel, press <Kbd>Alt</Kbd> + <Kbd>Q</Kbd> to search for any command.

## FAQ

**Why do some shortcuts not work?** Conflicts with browser extensions or system shortcuts. Try incognito mode or check browser shortcut settings.

**Can I create my own shortcuts?** Yes. File → Options → Customize Ribbon → Keyboard Shortcuts → Customize.

**Are these the same in Google Sheets?** Most navigation shortcuts are the same. Formula and formatting shortcuts differ significantly in Google Sheets.
