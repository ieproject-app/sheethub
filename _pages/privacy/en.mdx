## Introduction

SheetHub ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website at sheethub.web.id.

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected Information

When you visit SheetHub, certain information is automatically collected:

- **Usage Data**: pages visited, time spent, referral source, and interaction patterns.
- **Device Information**: browser type, operating system, and screen resolution.
- **IP Address**: used for analytics and security purposes.

### Information You Provide Voluntarily

We do not require user accounts to access content. If you contact us via email, we will retain your message and email address solely for responding to your inquiry.

## Cookies

SheetHub uses cookies and similar browser storage for:

- **Essential Functions**: theme preference (dark/light mode) and reading list items, stored locally in your browser.
- **Advertising**: third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
- **Analytics**: measurement cookies may be used to understand site usage — only with your consent.

When you first visit, a consent banner lets you choose between accepting all cookies or essential functions only. You can change your choice anytime by clearing your browser storage for this site.

## Advertising

SheetHub displays advertising served by **Google AdSense**.

- **Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads** based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
- **Google's use of advertising cookies** (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- You may **opt out of personalized advertising** by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- You can also manage how advertising cookies are used through your browser settings and via [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
- Where required by law (including the EEA and UK), personalized advertising only occurs after you give consent through our consent banner, and advertising-related storage remains denied until you do.

## Third-Party Services

SheetHub uses the following third-party services:

- **Firebase** (Google): website hosting and authentication. Firebase may collect anonymized usage data. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Google AdSense** (Google): advertising delivery and measurement, as described in the [Advertising](#advertising) section above.
- **Google Fonts**: for typography. See [Google Fonts Privacy](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).
- **Disqus**: for article comments. Disqus may set cookies and collect data — including for its own features and promotions — as described in their [Privacy Policy](https://help.disqus.com/en/articles/1717103-disqus-privacy-policy).

## Data Retention

Automatically collected analytics data is retained for a limited period and then anonymized. Contact correspondence is retained only as long as necessary to address your inquiry.

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- Access personal data we hold about you.
- Request deletion of your data.
- Opt out of personalized advertising via [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- Opt out of analytics tracking via browser settings.

To exercise these rights, contact us at **hermesthreads@gmail.com**.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.
