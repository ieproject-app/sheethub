## Introduction

SheetHub ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website at sheethub.web.id.

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected Information

When you visit SheetHub, certain information is automatically collected:

- **Usage Data**: pages visited, time spent, referral source, and interaction patterns.
- **Device Information**: browser type, operating system, and screen resolution.
- **IP Address**: used for analytics and security purposes.

### Information You Provide Voluntarily

We do not require user accounts to access content. If you contact us via email, we will retain your message and email address solely for responding to your inquiry.

## Cookies

SheetHub uses minimal cookies for:

- **Theme Preference**: stores your dark/light mode selection locally in your browser.
- **Reading List**: saves your reading list items locally in your browser.

We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes at this time.

## Third-Party Services

SheetHub uses the following third-party services:

- **Firebase** (Google): website hosting and authentication. Firebase may collect anonymized usage data. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Google Fonts**: for typography. See [Google Fonts Privacy](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).
- **Disqus**: for article comments. Disqus may set cookies and collect data as described in their [Privacy Policy](https://help.disqus.com/en/articles/1717103-disqus-privacy-policy).

## Data Retention

Automatically collected analytics data is retained for a limited period and then anonymized. Contact correspondence is retained only as long as necessary to address your inquiry.

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- Access personal data we hold about you.
- Request deletion of your data.
- Opt out of analytics tracking via browser settings.

To exercise these rights, contact us at **hermesthreads@gmail.com**.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

## Contact
