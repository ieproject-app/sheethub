## Get in Touch

Have a question about a tutorial? Found an error in one of our guides? Want to suggest a topic for a future article? We'd love to hear from you.

**Email:** hermesthreads@gmail.com

## Response Time

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 2-3 business days. For the fastest response, please include the article title or template name in your message.

## Before You Contact

- For formula troubleshooting, check the comments section on the relevant article first — another reader may have already found a solution.
- Template access issues are usually resolved by signing into a Google account and using File → Make a copy.
